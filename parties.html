QUESTIONS WELCOME

A few practical answers.

Where is the party held?

At OlaPono, 833 Main St W in Hartselle, Alabama.

How do we arrange a date?

Call 256-778-3051 with your preferred date and group size. Dates and services are subject to confirmation; this prototype cannot reserve them.

Does everyone have to receive treatment?

No. Each person decides individually. Any injectable treatment requires a private consultation, medical screening and informed consent.

Who gives the injections?

Every injectable treatment is given by our board-certified physician after an individual consultation and medical screening.

Are refreshments provided?

Yes, refreshments are part of the hosting concept. OlaPono provides non-alcoholic refreshments and does not serve alcohol. Ask us about bring-your-own guidelines and dietary needs. Anyone receiving injections should skip alcohol that day.

Is there a minimum group size or a package price?

Contact us for group-size requirements, availability and pricing. No minimum, package price or discount is assumed in this prototype.

Can guests under 18 attend?

No. These gatherings are for adults 18 and older.
