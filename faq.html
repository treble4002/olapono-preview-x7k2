A FEW PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Questions welcome.

Plain answers before you decide. Final clinical preparation and aftercare instructions come from your treating professional.

I’m new. Where should I start?

Browse Skin & Aesthetics, or contact the team to discuss the right starting point. The AI guides are optional; you can always speak with a person.

Do you offer services for teens?

We want age-appropriate skin care to feel welcoming. Individual services have age restrictions and consent requirements; contact the team about suitability and guardian involvement. Adult aesthetics and optimization are not teen pathways.

How much is tox?

The draft general range is $6–7/unit, with an example campaign offer of $6/unit. The applicable rate, conditions and units must be confirmed with the team. Total treatment cost depends on clinician assessment.

Why are filler prices not listed?

Lip filler and facial balancing are consultation-led. Suitability, the plan and the cost are discussed privately before you decide.

Is Optimize available now?

No. It is a planned future chapter. Scope, oversight, eligibility and options are still being established.

Can I host an injectable party at OlaPono?

Yes, contact the team to discuss dates and group details. You bring your people; we provide the refreshments and services. Adults 18+ only. Each injectable treatment requires individual consultation and medical screening. Alcohol is not served to anyone receiving injections. Explore injectable parties.

Can I book from this prototype?

Book buttons open the existing external Mangomint booking site and require internet. No booking is submitted by this prototype.

Are Kai and Nohea medical professionals?

No. They are AI guides, not clinicians. In this prototype their cards are not connected. Do not enter sensitive health information.

What should I do before an appointment?

Ask the treating team for instructions specific to your appointment. Preparation varies; do not rely on a general website as personal medical advice.

Where can I find rescheduling and cancellation policies?

The team must confirm the current policies and how they are displayed in booking. No policy or fee is invented in this prototype.
